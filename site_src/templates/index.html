Source and attribution

Values derive from the World Bank's Space2Stats – Monthly & Annual Black Marble Nighttime Lights (dataset {{ meta.source.catalog_id }}, v{{ meta.source.catalog_version }}, CC BY 4.0), which aggregates NASA's Black Marble VNP46A3/A4 radiance (Román et al. 2018) to World Bank ADM2 districts. This project aggregates those districts to ADM1 exactly on World Bank units, approximately on GADM 4.1 units through a graded crosswalk, and republishes the district data with corrected no-data handling.

Limitations

  • GADM values are approximate. Districts are assigned whole to the GADM 4.1 unit they overlap most; every unit carries a match grade. Only World Bank ADM1 values are exact aggregates.
  • Coverage varies. Clouds and summer daylight at high latitudes reduce valid pixels; monthly sums are biased down when coverage is low.
  • Official annual ≠ mean of months. NASA's annual composite and annual values derived from monthly data are separate products.
  • Associations, not effects. Nighttime lights correlate with economic activity; nothing here estimates causal effects.
  • Series end. Suomi NPP stopped delivering observations on 2 November 2026.
