Before you merge

  • Years. DOSE ends in {{ dose.panel_years[1] }}; lights start in 2012. The panel has {{ "{:,}".format(dose.panel_rows_2012_on) }} DOSE region-years from 2012, {{ "{:,}".format(dose.panel_rows_with_lights) }} with lights.
  • Absorbed units. Where a GADM unit is smaller than the source districts (e.g. Kiev City inside Kiev), its light sits in the neighbour: see gadm_unit_absorbs / xw_absorbs_units. Merge those regions on the economic side too.
  • Reused IDs. DOSE reuses some GADM IDs for different units (e.g. Portugal's NUTS 3 regions). These are left unlinked rather than joined by ID.
  • Quality filters. Use coverage, w_valid_estimated_share and the link fields to drop weak observations.
  • Association ≠ causation. Lights proxy activity; any elasticity you estimate is descriptive unless your design says otherwise.
