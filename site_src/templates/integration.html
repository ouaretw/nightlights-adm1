Before you merge

  • Years. DOSE ends in {{ dose.panel_years[1] }}; lights start in 2012.
  • Absorbed units. Where a GADM unit is smaller than the source districts (e.g. Kiev City inside Kiev), its light sits in the neighbour: see gadm_unit_absorbs / xw_absorbs_units, and merge those regions on the economic side too.
  • Boundary versions. DOSE uses GADM 3.6 identifiers; the GADM product uses GADM 4.1. Links are checked by name or geometry, never assumed from matching codes alone.
  • Quality filters. Screen on coverage, w_valid_estimated_share and the link fields.
  • Association, not causation. Any elasticity of GDP with respect to lights is descriptive unless your research design identifies it.
