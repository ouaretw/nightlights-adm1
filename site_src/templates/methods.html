Methods and validation

What was done to the source, what was checked, and where uncertainty remains. All figures on this page are produced by pipeline/validate.py and the automated tests (validation.json).

On this page: Source · Source properties · Boundaries & outlines · Validation · Aggregation rules vs simple collapses · Economic analysis · Limitations · Related products

1. Source

World Bank, Space2Stats – Monthly & Annual Black Marble Nighttime Lights (dataset {{ meta.source.catalog_id }}, v{{ meta.source.catalog_version }}, CC BY 4.0): NASA Black Marble radiance (15 arc-seconds) aggregated directly to 39,366 ADM2 units of the World Bank Official Boundaries, monthly {{ meta.coverage.months[0] }} – {{ meta.coverage.months[1] }} and annual {{ meta.coverage.years_official_annual[0] }}–{{ meta.coverage.years_official_annual[1] }}. The source documentation does not state the Black Marble collection (C1 or C2); we therefore do not either. SHA-256 of the files used is recorded in build_meta.json.

Continuity. Suomi NPP stopped delivering observations on 2 November 2026, so the VNP46 series that this source is built on will not extend much beyond its current end. Continuation will depend on NOAA-20 (VJ146) products, which this source does not use.

2. Source properties the method relies on (tested on every build)

  1. One row per district and period; each district carries exactly one ADM1 code (strict nesting). One row is missing in the source (Svalbard, July 2022).
  2. ntl_mean is a coverage-weighted mean over valid pixels and ntl_sum the matching sum. The weights are fractional (sum/mean is not an integer), so the valid weight w_valid = sum / mean is an identity, not an estimate, whenever the mean is positive. A provincial mean Σ sums / Σ w_valid is then the exact coverage-weighted mean of the union.
  3. When no pixel is valid, the source stores sum = 0 with a missing mean. We set such sums to missing; a zero in our files always means observed darkness. ({{ "{:,}".format(val.arithmetic.adm1_months_no_valid_data_now_NA) }} World Bank province-months that v1 showed as 0 are now missing.)
  4. For valid but completely dark rows (mean = sum = 0) the weight cannot be recovered and is estimated from the district's stable total weight; this is flagged (w_valid_estimated, w_valid_estimated_share).
  5. Quality shares sum to one over all pixels, so they are aggregated with total weights.
  6. n_gasflaring_locs is constant within each country — a national count — and is published as such.
  7. Medians and percentiles cannot be aggregated from district summaries; they are published at ADM2 only.

Map outlines (display only)

The source uses an older World Bank boundary vintage than the boundaries published today, and some ADM1 codes were reused for different units in between. A World Bank ADM1 unit is therefore drawn with today's outline only when code and name agree. Otherwise its outline is rebuilt as the union of the current ADM2 polygons that its own source districts were matched to — by construction, the area its values were summed over — never using a polygon claimed by two provinces and never overlapping an outline already drawn; it is flagged approximate when some districts could not be matched. Units with neither are not drawn and are listed below each map; their data are complete in every file. The status of every unit is in geo_match_wb_adm1.csv. ADM2 maps draw a district only when its code and name match today's layer. Map outlines are simplified for display and must not be used for spatial analysis.

5. Economic analysis

The Economic Analysis page is built by pipeline/build_analysis.py; all inputs and outputs are listed on the downloads page.

  • National lights are exact aggregates of the World Bank ADM1 rows (sum of sums; mean = Σ sum ÷ Σ valid weight). If any unit that contains pixels lacks a valid observation, the national value is missing, never a partial total. The source's China unit “Taiwan Sheng” is reported as Taiwan, matching the IMF and WDI economy definitions; other boundary conventions (e.g. Western Sahara within Morocco's units, Crimea, Abkhazia, northern Cyprus) are left as in the source and noted per row.
  • Joins use ISO3 codes only: IMF group names were parsed from the April 2023 Statistical Appendix (Tables B and E; 41 + 155 economies, counts asserted) and matched to ISO3 through the World Bank country list plus an explicit table; WDI values are joined on ISO3 and year. DOSE regions keep DOSE's GID_1; their lights come from the documented district links, not from names.
  • Logs are taken of positive values only; zero or missing values are excluded and counted. No constant is added.
  • Models are ordinary least squares on logs: log(total radiance) ~ log(population) + log(GDP per capita), by year (HC3 errors), pooled with year effects, and within country (country and year effects), with errors clustered by country. A Wald test asks whether the two elasticities are equal (total radiance scaling with GDP alone). Spatial dependence is checked with Moran's I of residuals on the five nearest country centroids (999 permutations).
  • Prediction checks: leave one World Bank region out and train on 2012–2018 / test on later years (countries); leave one country out and train 2012–2016 / test 2017–2019 (DOSE regions). Reported as RMSE in log points against a training-mean baseline.
  • No circularity: no GDP is distributed to provinces using lights, so nothing on the page validates lights against itself.

6. Known limitations

  • The GADM version is a crosswalk, not a raster re-extraction: districts are assigned whole, so units whose borders cut through source districts carry some misallocated light (graded and flagged).
  • The source uses an older World Bank boundary vintage; ADM1 codes were later reused for different units in some countries. A province is therefore drawn with today's outline only where code and name still agree; otherwise its outline is rebuilt as the union of the current district polygons its own source districts were matched to (the area its data were summed over), and flagged approximate when some districts could not be matched. Provinces with neither are not drawn (status of every unit: geo_match_wb_adm1.csv).
  • Monthly sums are underestimated when coverage is low (clouds; summer at high latitudes). Filter on coverage.
  • Medians/percentiles exist only at ADM2. Gas-flare variants are sums only at ADM1.
  • No raster-level re-validation was possible for every country: the direct-extraction benchmark covers 13 countries and three years.

Interpreting radiance in economic work

  • Nighttime radiance is not GDP. It reflects lighting technology and policy, infrastructure, settlement patterns and population as well as economic activity; the relation differs across countries and over time.
  • Mean and median measure different things. A mean is pulled up by a few bright cities; a median describes the typical pixel. Large rural units can have a near-zero median and a low mean despite substantial economic activity. Only means and sums are published at ADM1.
  • Observation effects: clouds, snow, aurora, stray light and atmospheric conditions reduce or bias valid observations; gas flares and fires add light unrelated to settlement; very bright cores can saturate or bloom across neighbouring pixels.
  • Products are not interchangeable: official annual composites, means of monthly composites, and other sensors (DMSP-OLS, NOAA-20) differ in processing.
  • Boundaries change: administrative units are split, merged and renumbered; identifiers from different boundary versions are not interchangeable.
  • Correlation is not causation; associations with GDP or population on the analysis page are descriptive.
  • Missing economic observations are never estimated or filled in; they stay missing and are counted.
